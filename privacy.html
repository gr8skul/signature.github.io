Signature Dispatch Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Signature Dispatch is an internal work-order scheduling and coordination application intended for authorized business personnel.

Information handled

The application may process customer contact details, service locations, work-order descriptions and statuses, scheduling details, and employee or technician contact information. Authorized users may also enter service notes and other work-related records.

How information is used

Information is used to organize service work, coordinate technicians, manage schedules, and maintain business records. Access is intended to be restricted according to user roles.

Google Calendar integration

Signature Dispatch uses Google Calendar to create and maintain scheduling events for work orders. Events may include a work-order identifier, customer name, work description, shop, schedule, status, and release status. Assigned technicians may be invited using their work email addresses. Event information is sent to Google to provide this functionality. The application does not use Google Calendar data for advertising.

Storage and access

Application records are stored using Supabase infrastructure. Calendar event information is processed using Google services. Authorized administrators manage user access. Information may be accessible to the service providers supporting these operations under their respective terms and privacy policies.

Data retention and requests

Business records are retained as needed for operations and applicable requirements. For questions about access, correction, or deletion of information, contact the organization administering Signature Dispatch.

Changes

This policy may be updated when application functionality or business practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
